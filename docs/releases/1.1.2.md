- Corrected character/item 3D previews, import scale and captured cape pose.
- Gold border follows native equip/unequip progress; slots move after native success.
- One gradual rune reveal for lore and guardian texts.
- Clear large map, clean Esc opening and restrained crafting border feedback.
- Stability corrections for recovery, ownership, input, inventory validation and reports.
