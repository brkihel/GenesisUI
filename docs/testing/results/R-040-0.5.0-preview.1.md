## Diego's comparison (in-game bars vs the hp-bars-new reference)

Agreed reading of the differences, in order of weight:

1. Silhouette: the reference is one slender continuous piece ending in points; ours looked
   like a box with a wider flat lid and base.
2. Dead space: the rail wood was so dark it merged with the opening, so the liquid seemed
   to float in a wide black gap.
3. Material: the reference is worn, weathered, with bright chipped edges; ours was smooth,
   low-contrast brown.
4. Rune: the reference's rune is light, in its own recessed panel; our V was tiny and dark.
5. Liquid: the reference is deep, calm red with a few long thin cracks; ours was pinkish,
   dense and contrasty, reading as noise.
6. Value plate: ours was much wider than the bar and cut the silhouette.
7. Channel: the reference has a bevelled lip around the liquid.

## Follow-up in 0.5.0-preview.2

- Carved bar frame redrawn as one piece (pointed cap and pommel, same width as the rails),
  scaled with the bar's width so it never stretches; bars slimmer (40 / 34 wide).
- Lighter weathered palette, worn edge highlight, blotches and chips on every carved piece;
  light V and diamond inlaid in recessed panels; dark-brown empty channel with a lip.
- Liquid: large soft clouds and three long thin cracks with small branches (both styles).
- Value plate barely wider than the bar; `[Vitals] ShowValues` hides it for clean bars.
