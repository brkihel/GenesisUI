## Proven composition defect

The source render texture uses 4× MSAA and bilinear filtering. Both can mix the
magenta clear colour into a silhouette sample before `GenesisUI/Keyed` removes it.
That shader derives alpha from colour distance and clamps it to 1 at distance 0.30.
For a half-covered black edge the mixed RGB is (0.5, 0, 0.5): its distance from the
key is 0.707, so the shader returns alpha 1 and leaves the pink mixture intact.
The current unmix step therefore cannot remove key colour from such an edge.

Next correction: avoid mixing key colour into the source samples; decode the key
at individual texel centres before interpolating premultiplied colour and coverage
for the UI. Use a bounded 2× source resolution instead of MSAA to keep edges smooth.
The next client build must confirm that the outline is gone and models stay visible.
