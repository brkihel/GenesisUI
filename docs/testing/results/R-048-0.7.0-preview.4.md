# R-048 — 0.7.0-preview.4

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-09-29 |
| Run by | Diego, own client; screenshots in `prints-tests-genesusui/GenesisMods-GenesisUI-0.7.0-preview.4/` |
| Result | Inventory behavior partly passed; visual layout and filter failed |

The three inventory panels opened; item descriptions, item handling, weight and protection
worked as implemented. The vanilla inventory cells had no GenesisUI frame. Clicking the filter
did not show category options. The top tab bar looked too tall, and the window covered too much
of the viewport. Mid-edge panel diamonds were misplaced and unwanted; the panel title divider
also needed the full tab marker, with the knot kept clear of text.

The central stamina bar was too small. The enemy health bar had black corners and no health
liquid. Diego supplied neutral replacement textures for the hotbar, slots, map and resource
bars. The eight separate thin slots from this build were liked and are preserved at
`hotbar-minimal-v1`; the green equipped glow was rejected. Preview.5 addresses these reports.

No `LogOutput.log` was provided for this run; the client behavior of preview.5 remains to be
checked in R-049.
